About the Film

That Friend is an indie bromance comedy about Paul (Harvey Guillen) — you know, that friend whose invitation for "one drink" seems harmless but inevitably leads to chaotic consequences.

When Paul crashes his best friend Henry's (Josh Brener) romantic weekend in Palm Springs with his new girlfriend Penny (Billie Lourd), what starts as an innocent visit spirals into mayhem after Paul's drug-laced cigarettes disappear into a stranger's hands.

Directed by the emerging filmmaking duo Will Sterling and Alex Wall in their feature debut, this film explores the hilarious and complex dynamics of friendship, loyalty, and the chaos we create for the people we love most.
